Who it's for

A small-business owner or manager

They use digital tools but do not have dedicated cybersecurity staff. CyberShield AI helps them see the most important risks and what to improve first.

Feel

Polished, simple, reassuring

A clear cybersecurity assessment product, with a visual dashboard that makes high-priority risks easy to understand without a lot of reading.

Why it matters

Make practical security accessible

Many small businesses rely on digital tools but lack dedicated cybersecurity staff to identify weaknesses and prioritize fixes. CyberShield AI aims to make risks understandable and next steps practical.

Now · prove the core
  • One end-to-end, self-reported assessment in a polished web experience.
  • Concise questions across enough areas to distinguish meaningful risks; exact count and coverage go in the PRD.
  • Transparent, rule-based score, risk identification, and priorities.
  • A visual dashboard that clearly surfaces the top risks.
  • AI explanations and recommendations grounded in assessment results; AI does not determine scores or priorities.
  • One complete journey demonstrable in about a minute.
Later · grow after the demo
  • A broader SaaS product for small businesses, after the assessment-to-action-plan value is proven.
Cut · keep this build small
  • A complete cybersecurity platform or SaaS product; the hackathon version proves one journey within the 2–4-hour active-work target.
  • Automated system or network scanning and a full technical audit; this proof relies on the owner's answers.
To settle in the PRD: question count and coverage, and the exact AI explanation and recommendation behavior. Keep the scoring methodology transparent and rule-based throughout.
