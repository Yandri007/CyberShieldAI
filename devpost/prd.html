What happens on each screen?

Expand a step to see the behavior and the evidence that demonstrates it.

1Landing · explain the value and start

Show the main message, 3–5-minute estimate, no-expertise promise, personalized overview and actions, the Assess → Understand → Act visual, and Start Assessment. No account/login.

Check: a first-time owner can identify what the app does and where to begin within seconds.
2Assessment · eight guided questions

One question at a time, with a progress count out of eight, plain language, Yes/No/Not sure choices where appropriate, short helper text, and Next/Back. The eight areas are Passwords, MFA, Backups, Updates, User Access, Network/Wi-Fi, Device Protection, and Employee Practices.

Check: all eight answers are required; Back preserves earlier answers; a blank is highlighted and never treated as Not sure.
3Results dashboard · show score and top risks

Label the value “Self-reported security-controls score.” Show the exact score, status, plain-language meaning, and highest-priority findings. State that it is based on answers, not a measured or verified security level; even a high score is not technical verification. Each risk shows its area, severity, why it matters, and whether it is a confirmed gap or needs verification. Sort deterministically by priority, confirmed No before Not sure, then fixed assessment order: Password security, MFA, Backups, Updates, User Access, Network/Wi-Fi, Device Protection, Employee Practices.

Check: the score equals the sum of eight fixed area scores; risk labels follow the matrix; the screen says results are self-reported and not a scan/audit.
4AI action plan · explain and guide, not chat

Before the AI call, the application determines the score, findings, severity, ranking, and risk-to-action mapping. AI receives those structured results and relevant assessment answers as context, then selects approved summary and explanation styles. Django renders all displayed explanations and actions from fixed copy. From Get AI Guidance, show up to three prioritized findings with what to do, why it matters, the risk addressed, and how to get started. AI cannot create findings or change scores, priorities, ranking, or action mapping.

Check: every remediation action points to a finding; preventive actions are labeled; the result is structured, not conversational.

What if?

Important result and error states

Open the cards to review how the product stays useful and trustworthy in each case.

Question unanswered

Identify and highlight it with “Please answer this question to continue.” On final submit, return to the first unanswered question. No automatic Not sure.

AI service unavailable

Keep the rules score and findings. Show “AI guidance is temporarily unavailable, but your security assessment is ready,” predefined risk-linked fallback actions, and a retry option.

Fewer than three findings

Show only findings that exist. Extra plan entries may be preventive or verification steps, clearly labeled.

No significant findings

Show a positive result based on self-reported answers; recommend maintenance/prevention without inventing a problem.
