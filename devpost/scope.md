# CyberShield AI

A polished, guided cybersecurity assessment that helps small-business owners understand their main risks and what to improve first.

## The Unique Kernel
CyberShield AI combines transparent, rule-based risk assessment with AI explanations and practical recommendations tailored to the results. The rules determine the score and priorities; AI makes those findings easier to understand and act on.

## Who It's For
A small-business owner or manager without dedicated cybersecurity staff. They rely on digital tools for daily work but may not know their biggest security weaknesses or which improvement to prioritize.

## The Core Loop
The owner completes a concise assessment about their business. A transparent rules-based method determines the security posture score, risks, and priorities. A polished dashboard makes the top risks easy to spot; AI explains the important findings in plain language and turns them into three practical actions to start with.

## Inspiration & Identity
Simple, professional, and reassuring, with the polish of a cybersecurity assessment product rather than a bare form and score. The dashboard should make top risks visually clear without requiring a lot of reading. The experience should be understandable without cybersecurity knowledge, not technical or intimidating.

## Why CyberShield AI Matters
Many small businesses rely on computers, email, Wi-Fi, and cloud services but do not have dedicated cybersecurity staff to identify weaknesses or decide what to fix first. CyberShield AI is intended to make those risks understandable and help owners take practical, prioritized steps.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a short demo, an owner completes concise questions spanning enough security areas to produce meaningful differences in risk; the exact question count is for the PRD. The app applies transparent rules to produce a posture score and prioritized risks, then shows the top risks clearly in a polished dashboard. AI does not score or set priorities: it explains the resulting findings in simple language and creates a personalized top three action plan with what to do, why it matters, and how to get started. The full assessment-to-action flow is demonstrable on screen in about one minute.

## The POC Boundary
- One end-to-end, self-reported cybersecurity assessment in a polished web experience.
- Concise questions covering enough areas to distinguish meaningful risks; choose the exact number and coverage in the PRD.
- Transparent, rule-based scoring, risk identification, and prioritization.
- A visually clear dashboard that surfaces the top risks.
- AI explanations and practical recommendations grounded in the assessment results; AI does not determine the score or priorities.
- A complete flow that can be demonstrated on screen in about one minute.

## Later
A broader SaaS product for small businesses, after the proof of concept demonstrates the core assessment-to-action-plan value.

## Explicitly Cut
- A complete cybersecurity platform or SaaS product. The hackathon version proves one assessment journey within the 2–4-hour active-work target.
- Automated system or network scanning and a full technical audit. The proof of concept is based on the owner's answers, keeping the assessment focused and demonstrable.
